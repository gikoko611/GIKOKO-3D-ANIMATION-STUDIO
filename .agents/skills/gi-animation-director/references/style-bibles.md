# G.I Animation Director — Style Bibles

Choose one primary visual language per project. Mix styles only when the brief requires it. These are original production notes, not copied third-party assets.

## Supported styles
1. **2D Cartoon** — bold silhouettes, clean contours, limited palette, squash-and-stretch; use layered SVG, Canvas or Pillow.
2. **3D Cartoon** — exaggerated proportions, rounded forms, expressive faces, soft key/fill/rim lighting and reusable deformation rigs.
3. **Stickman** — minimal line-figure design, consistent joint positions, strong posing, anticipation, contact points and foot planting.
4. **Anime-inspired 2D** — strong key poses, held frames, smear frames, speed lines and impact frames; use original character designs.
5. **Low-poly 3D** — deliberate polygon budget, flat or weighted normals, simple materials and stylized lighting.
6. **Clay / Stop-motion** — soft imperfect surfaces and stepped motion; avoid distracting random jitter.
7. **Pixel Art** — fixed pixel grid, nearest-neighbor scaling, restricted palette and integer camera movement.
8. **Paper Cut-out** — layered body parts with named pivots, rotation, translation, scale and parallax.
9. **Motion Graphics** — typography, icons, charts, masks, path motion and clear visual hierarchy.
10. **Isometric Diorama** — orthographic camera, consistent grid, controlled depth and shadow direction.
11. **Dark Fantasy** — high-contrast silhouettes, restrained accents, atmospheric layers and motivated lighting.
12. **Retro PS1 / Retro 3D** — low-resolution textures, limited geometry and deliberate retro artifacts.
13. **Realistic PBR** — physically based materials, plausible roughness/metalness, calibrated lighting and restrained camera motion.
14. **Frosted Glass / Abstract Product** — translucent layers, controlled reflections, soft gradients and legible typography.

## Style selection checklist
- What emotion should the audience feel?
- What is the smallest playback screen?
- Does the style fit the runtime and render budget?
- Can the same character be reproduced in every shot?
- Are fonts, textures, models and sounds licensed for the intended use?

## Style lock
Record style name, palette, outline width, material rules, lighting recipe, camera projection, texture resolution and motion personality in the project bible. Do not silently change these between shots.
